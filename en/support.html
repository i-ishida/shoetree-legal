Reporting a Bug

When reporting a bug or unexpected behavior, it helps to include the following:

  • Your device (e.g. iPhone 15) and OS version
  • The steps that led to the issue
  • A screenshot, if possible

Deleting Your Data

You can delete your account and all of your data at any time from Settings → Account within the App. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Canceling Your Subscription

To cancel a Pro subscription, use the account settings of the store through which you purchased (App Store or Google Play). It cannot be canceled from within the App.
